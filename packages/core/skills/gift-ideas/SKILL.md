# Gift ideas

1. Missing recipient, occasion, or budget: call ask_user once (prefilled) and end your reply.
2. Call shop once with 4-8 specific ideas as short catalog searches (e.g. "wireless earbuds under 50"), at the user's named stores or the regional general-merchandise defaults. Mix price points inside the budget.
3. Show picks in a show_ui cards block (default square shape): product, price, store as source, and product link, with one short reason each in the subtitle. A single product is also a card with its picture, price, and link.
4. Tailor to what the user said about the recipient and their own interests; don't repeat the profile back.
5. Reply with the top pick and why in one sentence. Don't claim to have bought or added anything.
