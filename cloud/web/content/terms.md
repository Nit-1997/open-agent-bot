# Terms of Service

These terms cover the hosted Open Agent service. The open-source app you run on your own Mac is covered by its MIT licence instead.

## The service

Open Agent is a personal research agent. It searches the web, reads pages, runs tools and writes reports for you, using the model you pick.

The hosted service is a free demo. It can change, slow down or stop at any time, and it comes with no guarantee that it is available or that its answers are correct. Check anything important yourself.

## Your account

- You sign in with Google or with an email link. Keep access to that account safe; anything done while signed in counts as yours.
- One person per account. Don't share it, sell it or create accounts in bulk.
- You can delete your account at any time from your profile.

## Fair use and limits

Every account gets the same request limits: how many chats can run at once, how many requests per hour and how many per month. A request is one message that starts the agent. The current numbers are shown next to your usage meter.

When you reach a limit, the agent stops taking new requests until the limit resets. If you need more, run Open Agent on your own Mac, where there are no limits.

## What you may not do

- Try to get around the limits, the human check or the sign-up rules, for example with throwaway email addresses or many accounts.
- Attack, overload or probe the service, or use it to reach private networks.
- Use the agent to collect other people's personal data at scale.

We may suspend or delete accounts that break these rules.

Your chats, reports and settings stay yours. We store them only to run the service for you, as described in the Privacy Policy.

Your messages are sent to the model provider you choose. Their own terms apply to what they do with them.
